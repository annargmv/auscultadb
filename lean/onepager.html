Proposal

A pilot that fits a longevity clinic

  1. Record. Add a 60-second recording at four chest sites, with one digital stethoscope model, to visits where an ECG is already taken. Each visit yields resting heart rate, a murmur screen and a wheeze check, plus paired stethoscope–ECG data that settles adult heart-rate variability on that device.
  2. Repeat. Record again at each follow-up and average several clips per visit, so that a change can be read against the measured noise rather than a single clip.
  3. Link. Relate the acoustic measures to the clinic's biological-age measures and to later outcomes. No open auscultation dataset links recordings to outcomes today; this is the step that would turn measurements into risk markers.
