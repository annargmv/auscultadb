    Path to a longevity marker

    1. Calibrate the murmur flag for each adult stethoscope. The network ranks adult valve disease well; its flag level, set on children, does not carry over between devices. Calibrate on one part of each adult database and report on another.
    2. Train the beat finder on adult beats. Adult heart-rate variability just missed its pre-set rule against the ECG. ECG-timed adult recordings give the training labels.
    3. Link recordings to outcomes. A cohort with stethoscope recordings, ECG and follow-up (events, deaths) would test sound-derived heart rate and heart-rate variability as risk markers, not only as measurements. No open dataset has that today.
